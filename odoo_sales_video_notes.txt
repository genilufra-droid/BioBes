    *   **Kanban View:** Displays quotations as cards organized by their stage in the sales process, providing a visual overview of the pipeline.

### Sales Workflow: Quotations to Orders
*   **Creation:** Clicking the "New" button opens a form view to create a quotation.
*   **Form Details:** The form includes essential fields such as Customer, Invoice/Delivery Addresses, Expiration Date, Pricelist, and Payment Terms. 
*   **Quotation Templates:** Pre-defined templates can be used to automatically populate order lines with standard products, services, and terms, ensuring consistency and saving time.
*   **Order Lines:** Users can add products individually or use the "Catalog" feature for bulk selection. Order lines can be organized using sections and notes. 
*   **Quote Builder:** This feature allows users to design professional PDF quotations with customizable headers, footers, and product descriptions.
*   **Workflow Actions:** A status bar at the top tracks progress: **Quotation -> Quotation Sent -> Sales Order**. Buttons allow for sending the quote by email, confirming it into a Sales Order, or creating a pro-forma invoice.

### Customer and Address Management
*   **Customer Profiles:** Customers can be categorized as individuals or companies. Profiles store contact information, tax IDs, and internal notes.
*   **Multiple Addresses:** Odoo supports multiple address types for a single customer, including Contact, Invoice, Delivery, and "Other" (e.g., branch offices). This ensures that documents are sent to the correct locations automatically.

### Invoicing and Payment
*   **Invoicing Policies:** Invoicing can be triggered based on "Ordered Quantities" or "Delivered Quantities."
*   **Invoice States:** The invoicing workflow moves through **Draft -> Posted -> Paid**. 
*   **Integration:** Once an order is confirmed, an invoice can be generated with a single click. Payments can be recorded directly against the invoice, and the system supports automatic invoicing upon online payment confirmation.

### Advanced Features and ERP Design Considerations
*   **Pricelists and Discounts:** Support for complex pricing strategies, including fixed prices, percentage discounts, and loyalty programs.
*   **Shipping Connectors:** Integration with major carriers (DHL, FedEx, UPS) to calculate shipping costs and track deliveries directly within the sales order.
*   **External Connectors:** Synchronization with e-commerce platforms like Amazon and Shopee, and print-on-demand services like Gelato.
*   **Reporting:** A dedicated Reporting section offers deep dives into sales performance by salesperson, product, or customer, using various chart types and pivot tables.
*   **Commissions:** Management of sales team targets and commission plans based on achieved revenue.

For an ERP designer, Odoo 19 demonstrates the importance of a unified yet flexible data model, where information flows seamlessly between modules (Sales to Inventory to Invoicing) while providing users with highly configurable views and automated workflows to minimize manual data entry.
