Odoo 19's bank reconciliation and accounting interface is designed for efficiency, featuring a clean, card-based dashboard and a streamlined workflow for matching bank statements with internal records.

### Dashboard Journal Cards
The **Accounting Dashboard** serves as the central hub, presenting bank and credit card accounts as individual cards. Each card displays key metrics:

*   **Action Buttons:** Direct links to "Bank Setup," "Transactions," and a dynamic "X to reconcile" button that highlights the number of pending items.
*   **Status Overview:** Visual indicators for sales and purchases, showing amounts that are "To Validate," "Unpaid," or "Late."

### Transaction List & Status Cues
Clicking into a journal reveals the **Transaction List**, which uses color-coded cues to indicate reconciliation status:
*   **Unreconciled Transactions:** Displayed at the top under "Current Balance" with prominent **purple action buttons** suggesting the next logical step (e.g., "Set Partner," "Reconcile," "Receivable").
*   **Reconciled Transactions:** Marked with a **green checkmark** and the name of the matched record (e.g., "INV/2025/00050").
*   **Detailed View:** Each line can be expanded to show the specific journal items involved, with an "info" icon providing a summary of the match.

### Matching Panel & Action Controls
When manual intervention is needed, the **Matching Panel** ("Search: Journal Items to Match") opens a focused view:
*   **Search & Filter:** Users can search for specific partners or labels to find matching invoices or bills.
*   **Live Balance:** A real-time balance indicator at the top right updates as items are selected, helping users reach a zero balance for perfect reconciliation.
*   **Action Menu:** A vertical ellipsis menu on each transaction line provides advanced options like "Internal Transfers," "Cash Discount," "Manage Models," or "Upload Bills" for on-the-fly record creation.

### Filters & Navigation
The interface includes robust filtering and grouping options to manage high volumes of data:
*   **Breadcrumbs:** Easy navigation between Dashboard, Journals, and specific entries.
*   **Search Bar:** Supports complex queries and saved "Favorites."
*   **Filters:** Quick toggles for "Posted," "Unposted," "To Check," and specific journals (Bank, Cash, Sales, etc.).

### Reconciliation Workflow
1.  **Import/Sync:** Bank transactions are brought in via synchronization or manual upload.
2.  **Automatic Matching:** Odoo's default rule automatically matches transactions if two of three elements (Partner, Label, Amount) align with an existing record.
3.  **Suggested Actions:** For remaining items, the system suggests actions based on partner history or open documents.
4.  **Manual Reconciliation:** Users select "Reconcile" to manually pair transactions with one or more invoices/bills in the matching panel.
5.  **Handling Discrepancies:** Small differences (e.g., bank fees) can be handled by setting a "Tolerance" or manually allocating the remainder to a specific account (like "Miscellaneous Operating Charges").
6.  **Validation:** Once matched, the "Suspense Account" entry is replaced by the actual counterpart account (e.g., "Account Receivable"), and the transaction is marked as reconciled.
7.  **Editing:** Reconciled items can be "unreconciled" using the trash icon or edited using the pencil icon to adjust payment types (Partial vs. Full).
