4.  **Inventory Integration**: A "Receipt" **Smart Button** appears at the top of the PO, linking directly to the **Inventory app**. Clicking "Receive" or the Smart Button opens the warehouse receipt form, where users can "Validate" the incoming shipment to update stock levels.
5.  **Vendor Bills**: Odoo 19 streamlines invoicing through the "Upload Bill" feature. Users can upload a digital copy of the vendor's invoice, and Odoo creates a draft bill. 6.  **Product History**: Successfully completed purchases automatically update the "Purchase" tab on the **Product Form**, recording the vendor and the last unit price for future reference and automated replenishment.
